Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 28, 2026

FaxDaddy ("App") is operated by Moonstone Internet Ventures ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and being transparent about how we handle your data. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your information.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you sign in with Apple, we receive your Apple user identifier and, if you choose to share it, your name and email address. Apple may provide a private relay email address if you choose to hide your email.

Fax Content: Documents, images, and files you upload for faxing are temporarily stored on our encrypted servers for the purpose of fax transmission. Vault documents saved within the App are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to our servers.

Fax Metadata: We store information about your fax transmissions, including recipient fax numbers, send/receive timestamps, delivery status, page count, and confirmation codes.

Contacts: If you choose to save fax contacts within the App, we store the names and fax numbers you provide. If you import from your device's contacts, we only access the specific contacts you select — we do not upload or store your entire address book.

Device Information: We collect your device push notification token to send you delivery confirmations and other service notifications. We collect standard analytics data through Firebase Analytics, including device type, OS version, app version, and usage patterns.

Payment Information: All purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect, store, or have access to your credit card number, bank account, or other payment details.

3. Where Your Data Lives

FaxDaddy uses two distinct storage approaches depending on the type of data. We believe in keeping your documents on your device whenever possible.

Stored on your device (never uploaded):

  • Vault documents saved in "My Documents" — stored locally using iOS Data Protection (NSFileProtectionComplete), encrypted by iOS when your device is locked, and never transmitted to our servers
  • Imported files and scanned documents before you send them
  • Your app preferences and settings

Stored on our encrypted US servers:

  • Fax documents after transmission — retained in your Sent History until you delete them
  • Received faxes — stored until you delete them
  • Account information (Apple ID, name, fax number)
  • Fax metadata (recipient numbers, timestamps, delivery status)
  • Contacts you create within the App

4. Data Security

Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between the App and our servers is encrypted using TLS 1.3.

Encryption at Rest: Documents stored on our servers are encrypted using AES-256 server-side encryption. Our servers are located in the United States.

Access Controls: All API requests are authenticated via JSON Web Tokens (JWT). Every database query is filtered by your user ID — you can never access another user's data. Document access uses time-limited pre-signed URLs that expire after 15 minutes.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes. We share data only with the following service providers, solely to operate the App:

  • Telnyx — Fax transmission via the Public Switched Telephone Network (PSTN)
  • Amazon Web Services (S3) — Encrypted document storage for fax transmission
  • RevenueCat — Subscription and purchase management
  • Firebase (Google) — Analytics and push notifications
  • Apple — Authentication (Sign in with Apple) and payment processing

Each provider processes data only as necessary to perform their service and is subject to their own privacy policies.

6. Data Retention

Fax documents: Documents uploaded for fax transmission are retained on our servers until you delete them from your Sent History. You may delete individual faxes at any time from within the App.

Vault documents: Stored locally on your device only. Deleted when you remove them from the Vault or delete the App.

Account data: Retained as long as your account is active. Upon account deletion, all associated data (fax history, contacts, documents) is permanently deleted within 30 days.

Received faxes: Retained on our servers until you delete them. We do not automatically delete received faxes.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data — view your fax history, contacts, and account information within the App
  • Delete your data — delete individual faxes, contacts, and vault documents within the App, or request full account deletion by contacting us
  • Correct your data — update your sender name and contact information within the App
  • Export your data — download your fax documents and delivery receipts from within the App
  • Opt out of push notifications via your device's notification settings

To exercise these rights or request full account deletion, contact us at privacy@getfaxdaddy.com.

8. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • The right to know what personal information we collect and how it is used
  • The right to request deletion of your personal information
  • The right to opt out of the sale of personal information — we do not sell your personal information
  • The right to non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights

9. Children's Privacy

FaxDaddy is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

10. App Tracking Transparency

FaxDaddy requests your permission before enabling any tracking, in compliance with Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework. You may decline tracking with no impact on the App's functionality. If you allow tracking, we use this data solely to measure the effectiveness of our advertising campaigns.

11. HIPAA Disclaimer

FaxDaddy uses industry-standard encryption to protect your documents. However, FaxDaddy is not a HIPAA-covered entity and does not sign Business Associate Agreements (BAAs). If you handle Protected Health Information (PHI), you are responsible for determining whether FaxDaddy meets your compliance requirements.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy within the App and updating the effective date above. Your continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance.
